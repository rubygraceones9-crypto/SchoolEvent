Section 2 — Project Concept & Idea

What is your project called? (working title is fine)
School Event Hub

Describe your project in 2–3 sentences.
School Event Hub is a professional digital media distribution platform designed for high-resolution asset management in educational environments. It provides centralized, event-specific media hubs with automated QR code generation and download tracking to solve the problem of unorganized and low-quality photo sharing during school functions.

What is the ONE core feature your app will demonstrate?
A centralized media repository featuring automated QR code generation for instant event-specific photo access and high-definition downloads.
